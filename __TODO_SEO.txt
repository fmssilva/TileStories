SEO Strategy for Your Website
1. Google Business Profile (Critical!)

Claim and optimize your Google Business listing
This appears in "museums near me" searches
Include: accurate hours, photos, location, categories

2. Local SEO Focus

Target "Lisboa" and "Lisbon" variations
Include neighborhood names (Belém, Alfama, etc.)
Optimize for "near me" searches

3. Content Strategy
Create blog posts/pages about:

"How AR transforms museum visits in Lisbon"
"Interactive digital experiences at [your museum]"
"Family-friendly tech museums in Portugal"
"Behind the scenes: Creating digital museum twins"
Comparison pages vs traditional museums

4. Technical SEO Must-Haves

Mobile-friendly design (most searches are mobile)
Fast loading speed
Schema markup for events
Image optimization with alt text
Video content (highly engaging)

5. AI Agent Optimization
For AI agents (like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity) to recommend your museum:

Create clear, structured content about your unique AR/VR features
Use FAQ sections with natural language
Include detailed descriptions of what makes your experience unique
Mention specific technologies: "augmented reality," "digital twin," "gamification"
List concrete benefits: "identify objects in historical panels," "zoom into street views," "time-travel through Lisbon's history"

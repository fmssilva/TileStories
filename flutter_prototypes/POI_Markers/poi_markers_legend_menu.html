Religious
~67 POI
Church
Parish churches, cathedrals. From Sé de Lisboa to riverside chapels. Dominant type in the panorama.
Convent / Monastery
Jerónimos, Carmo (famous ruin preserved), Santos, São Francisco, Trindade.
Chapel / Hermitage
Small riverside chapels, roadside shrines. Many vanished completely after 1755.
Royal / Noble
~26 POI
Royal Palace
Paço da Ribeira (destroyed 1755), Palácio de Belém. Political and symbolic centre of Portugal.
Noble Palace / Quinta
Aristocratic estates, especially dense in Alcântara and Belém riverfront. Many rebuilt after earthquake.
Noble Garden / Estate
Formal gardens attached to quintas. Some still exist, many paved over in reconstruction.
Military
~14 POI
Tower / Fortified tower
Torre de Belém, watch towers, gate towers of old Fernandine walls.
Castle / Citadel
Castelo de São Jorge (survived), smaller hilltop fortifications.
Fort / Coastal battery
Tejo river defensive forts. Some submerged post-1755 by tsunami/silting.
Civic
~28 POI
Residential block
Dense medieval housing. Alfama, Mouraria. Lower city (Baixa) entirely destroyed and rebuilt on grid.
Square / Praça
Terreiro do Paço, Rossio, smaller neighbourhood squares. Heart of civic life.
Hospital / Almshouse
Todos-os-Santos hospital (destroyed). Welfare institutions of 18th-century Lisbon.
Maritime
~15 POI
Quay / Landing dock
Cais do Sodré area, Terreiro do Paço waterfront. Gateway for the Age of Discovery.
Shipyard / Arsenal
Naval yards at Alcântara and Ribeira. Critical infrastructure for Portuguese empire.
Fishing village / Beach
Praia de Belém, small fishing communities. Largely vanished after 1755 tsunami.
Infrastructure
~18 POI
Bridge
Roman bridge of Alcântara (visible clearly in panorama). Smaller creek crossings throughout city.
City Gate
Medieval Fernandine wall gates. All destroyed after 1755; the wall itself demolished in reconstruction.
Fountain / Mill
Neptune fountain at Terreiro do Paço. Tidal mills of Alcântara. Pottery kilns of Mocambo.
Landscape
~9 POI
Hill / Viewpoint
Seven hills of Lisbon. Colina do Castelo, Santa Catarina, Chiado hilltop. Shape the city's silhouette.
Stream / Ribeira
Ribeira de Alcântara, now underground. Valleys and watercourses visible in panorama topography.
Commerce
~9 POI
Market / Merchant house
Riverside market stalls, warehouses. Casa da Índia — hub for spices from the Orient.
Workshop / Manufactory
Pottery workshops of Mocambo — visible by chimneys. Produced the azulejo tiles seen in this panorama.

# POI Detail Card: look, Editor and behaviour (3.3)

This guide holds the target design of the POI detail card and of its Editor, part by part, as the Block Components Lab shows it.
For each part it says what Unity has today, where that code lives, and what is still missing. It covers everything `_3.1` (blocks)
and `_3.2` (navigation) draw.

- **The target is the Lab:** https://claude.ai/artifact/J9J4hWDvF161Bog9dsm83K. Its record copy is
  `_3.3.1_Prototype/Block Components Lab.html`, and `_3.3.1_Prototype/README.md` says what it shows and what to judge.
- **How this guide stays current:**
  - A correction the developer makes in a Lab round is written here the same round, as a rule in its component's section.
  - When Unity builds a part, that part's **Unity today** line changes.
  - No logs: git keeps the history, and `TestEvidence/` keeps the measurements and captures.
- **Status words:**
  - **Built:** Unity does it as the target says.
  - **Partly:** Unity does some of it; the line says what is missing.
  - **Not yet:** only the Lab has it.
- **Values:** the option lists and defaults are the decided ones. A value marked **(Lab)** exists only in the Lab and is confirmed in
  the real scene before it becomes a default (`00-process.md`, "Defaults are judged in the real scene").
- **Old names:** code comments cite old section names of this guide ("_3.3 P4.4a", "_3.3 S4a"). Section 19 says where each one is now.

## 1. Fixed rules

These hold for every part. No Editor option can break them.

- **D1 Legible in the sun first.**
  - All text is at least 7:1 against its ground, in both modes, with solid ink (no ink made with alpha).
  - Words on a picture (the hero's words, the bar's labels over the hero) are at least 4.5:1 against their own shade, at the
    worst pixel.
  - Icons, input borders and focus marks are at least 4.5:1; the bar's icons are at least 4.5:1, its labels 7:1.
  - Body text also passes APCA Lc 75.
  - Each step is proven twice: by arithmetic tests on the theme, AND by measuring the rendered pixels, plus a look at the render.
    A 35 % glare layer render is judged in both modes.
- **D2 One block, one surface.** A block's edge reads against the ground at 3:1 or more, measured on the render over photos of a
  sunny wall. Nothing of a block lives outside it.
- **D3 Scent before opening.** Every preview shows what it is and what opens it (title, format line such as "12 photos" or
  "Audio · 3:11", and the Open Row).
- **D4 A screen is never a wall.** The card is a calm list of previews; depth is one tap away, on the block's own page. No preview is
  taller than the preview cap (section 8.4).
- **D5 One focal point.** At most one accent-filled action in view, and one glowing element per card.
- **D6 Motion explains, never decorates text.**
  - Nothing advances by itself by default (WCAG 2.2.2).
  - Motion never delays reading: content is usable at once.
  - Reduce Motion is a rule, not a per-row option (section 9.1).
- **D7 Built from the system.** Every size is on the 4 / 8 pt scale and every value is a token. Asymmetry exists only as a named
  variant.
- **D8 The camera stays present, never behind text.** After the hero the sheet's ground is solid paper; text sits only on opaque
  surfaces or on its own shade.
- **Light is the default mode**, with a visitor switch (Settings panel).
- **The project renders in linear colour.** An alpha blend looks much lighter than in a browser: every scrim, fade and tint is
  calibrated by measuring the pixel behind it, never by its number.
- **The trend map.** Soft "neumorphic" relief is used only on things a finger drags (the before / after knob, the sheet grabber, a 3D
  handle). A gradient is used only on the one focal element and on picture shades. The display face is for the hero title and
  section openers only. One azulejo motif is used once.

## 3. The Editor's organisation

**Five tabs:** Global Scene, Specific Marker, Detail Card, the new **Guide** (after Detail Card) and the new **Languages & Terms**.

**Rules that hold in every tab:**
- **By UI element.** Every element the phone shows has ONE component foldout, named as it is on the phone.
- **Opening a foldout shows that element on the phone:** the right view, scrolled to it, outlined for a moment.
- **Inside a foldout:**
  1. the element's options;
  2. then an **Aa** button with its words (one row per word, named by what it is on the phone, one field per wall language);
  3. then **Demo content**, where the element has content.
- **A word is edited where its element's options are**, never in a broad section. A word shared by many elements lives on their
  common parent (the missing-media notice lives on the Media elements).
- **Inheritance reads as words:** "As configured" (from Detail Card) and "As the block" (from the block's defaults), with the
  inherited value in brackets, until the developer overrides it.
- **Every option has a (?) help**; conditional rows are indented; every tab ends with its live `config.json` preview.
- **PROPOSED** marks an option neither Unity nor the Lab had, added from the audit; the developer judges each one.
- **Foldout behaviour** (an Editor preference, not saved to the wall): many open or one at a time; open to level 0-3; a find box and
  category chips.

## 4. Foundations: theme, type, contrast

**Built.** The rest of the card stands on this.

- **One source.** `ThemeAsset` (`Resources/TileStories/Themes/Theme.default.asset`) holds every colour and theme scale for both modes.
  - `ThemeUssGenerator` writes `Runtime/UI/Theme/Generated/Theme.default.uss` (committed); `ThemeUssStaleTests` fails when it is
    stale.
  - `ThemeController` puts `.ts-app` + theme + mode classes on every UIDocument root; switching mode swaps one class and raises
    `ModeChanged`.
  - C# and UGUI read colours only through `ThemeResolver` (by name; `TryNumber`, `TryPixels`), never from USS.
- **Three tiers.**
  - T1 primitives: spacing 4/8/12/16/24/32/40/48, radius 6/12/16/20/24/30/pill, durations 120/220/360/500 ms + ambient 5/13/26 s,
    ramps, easing names, type sizes.
  - T2 semantic roles: surface, ink, accent, line, family colours, glass, metal, scrims, elevation, font roles.
  - T3 component tokens live in the component's own USS and default to T1 / T2. `CardTokens.uss` keeps only the card's own tokens
    (no colour); the numbers C# reads stay on `.ts-card`.
- **Two modes, light (default) and dark,** with the same 55 colour names (the generator refuses modes that differ). Mode
  precedence (`ThemeModeRule`): the visitor's pick > the wall's Default Mode > the theme's.
- **Wall knobs are closed lists**, applied as classes: theme, default mode, accent from a curated pre-measured list (heritage,
  azulejo, terracotta, garden), roundness (sharp / soft / round), motion level (full / reduced). There is no free colour picker:
  a free colour cannot be proven at 7:1. Their rows are in Card › Look and Motion (sections 6.2, 9.1).
- **Fonts.**
  - Display = Fraunces SemiBold (wght 600, opsz 28); body = Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400 / 500 / 700. OFL, in `Runtime/UI/Theme/Fonts/`.
  - Each face has a TMP asset and a static TextCore asset from the same `.ttf`, rebuilt by TileStories > Fonts > Rebuild Theme Font
    Atlases (`FontAtlasBuilder`). The display asset is filled at 600 and 700, so a bold title never gets a synthesized bold.
  - Each UI face has a **halo twin** (padding 18 / 2048) used only by the hero's back labels (`--ts-font-*-halo`). The normal faces
    stay at padding 6 / 1024, because a wide SDF greys every body word.
  - The charset is ASCII + Latin-1 + Latin Extended-A + typographic marks + every shipped character (`ShippedTextGlyphTests`).
- **Type scale:** hero 46 (40 under 400 pt), page title 32, plate title 24, block and section title 20, lede 19, body 17, caption 15,
  label 14, minimum 13. Line height 1.4-1.55 body, 1.05-1.1 display. TextCore's line height is a percentage of the FACE's own line:
  `--ts-el-leading` holds the converted value.
- **Measured contrast (light / dark), the floor every change keeps:**

  | Pair | Light | Dark |
  |---|---|---|
  | ink on surface | 18.82 | 15.43 |
  | ink-2 | 11.90 | 11.46 |
  | ink-3 | 9.13 | 8.93 |
  | accent text | 10.36 | 9.48 |
  | hero words on the scrim over pure white | 9.34 | 7.75 |
  | block edge over the sunny wall | 3.58 | 3.21 |

  Full tables: `TestEvidence/Theme/contrast_<mode>.txt`; tests `ThemeContrastTests`, `ThemeTypeTests`.
- **The block edge rule.** One rim colour cannot clear 3:1 against both a dark and a bright wall, so the edge is the rim line OR the
  surface beside it: light = a dark line #433C32 from ground luminance 0.24; dark = a light-metal line #CFC6B5 below 0.13.
- **Block emphasis** (`emphasis` on a block: normal / featured / quiet; `BlockFrame` in `PoiCard.uss`) draws in the **Cards** look:
  featured = deeper shadow + larger title, quiet = hairline + smaller title. Shadows vanish in dark mode, so dark elevation is rim +
  surface tone.

## 5. Preview & Test

Editor tools. Nothing here is ever in the app.

- **Demo card** (one component for the whole demo; the reference comes first, the full card after it):
  - **Tile on every card:** **On** / Off. A tile first in the first section of EVERY marker's card, in Scene and Play only
    (never in a build); it opens **Demo Components**. Words: `test_tile_tag`, `test_tile_title`, `test_tile_line`.
  - **Demo Components:** the page with every motion, interaction, effect, element and pattern, on real parts. Its first thing is
    the **Demo card** button (`test_demo_title`, `test_demo_line`); its Back returns to the card it came from.
  - **Demo card:** a card of its own with every block kind once, each with its demo content (filled once with the Ribeira
    Palace's real content, independent since: removing a block from a real point never touches it). It has no tile of its own.
    Its X returns to where it was opened from (Demo Components, a card at its stop, or the wall); its settings mark opens the
    settings panel as on any card. Words: `democard_title`, `democard_tag`.
  - Saved answers: Forget saved answers (section 6.6).
  - A block's look, motion, transitions and page live once, in Detail Card; the demo card and every marker draw them with their
    own content.
  - Tests: the demo card's content is the golden card; the tile's switch never changes a test (each test sets its own), and one
    test proves the tile shows on any marker when On and never in a build.
- **Editor tools on the phone share one look:** red dashes and an EDITOR tag (the demo card tile too), never the app's look, and
  a grip that moves a floating tool off what is being judged. The phone carries no view label; the page has no intro or step
  buttons above it (the setup order is a note at the top of the Detail Card tab).
- **Preview bar.** Floating on the phone (Shown by default): Light / Dark, Motion / Still, the language, Focus (the phone jumps to
  what is edited and dims the rest), the screen (phone, wide, tablet). Words: `pbar_*`.
- **A change redraws in place.** Any option, a preview-bar switch or a visitor setting changes only what it changes: the phone keeps
  its scroll, the hero its height, and what is on screen stays shown (no entrance replays); Light / Dark only repaints. The Lab's
  `test8f.js` measures it (no frame moves).
- In the Lab the demo card holds every kept kind once, with its options drawn; `#dcshot=<kind>` opens it at that block, still.
- **Unity today: not yet.** The tests' golden card is the Ribeira Palace (`ReferenceCard`, section 15). The demo card replaces it
  as the golden card, so curating the Ribeira Palace can never break a test.

## 6. Card

- **6.1 Sheet.**
  - Enabled (Off: a tap still selects the marker, no card opens).
  - Opens at: Peek / **Half**. It never opens Full by itself, so the wall stays in view (D8).
  - Half height max: 0.30-0.60, **0.40** (the measured Half, 337.6 of 844 pt).
  - Full height: **To the top** / Leave a camera strip (48 pt short, `--ts-sheet-top-gap`).
  - Tap outside closes: **On** / Off. A tap on a marker still selects it; a drag never closes; the X and a swipe down always close.
  - Phone's Back button (Android): **Steps the card down** / Left to the app. Each press undoes the innermost thing: a full view, a
    list, the row opened last, then one height down, then close.
  - Stops at 390 x 844: Peek 57 (the title row only, the camera stays clear), Half 337.6, Full 796 (`SheetStopRule`). A swipe up at
    Peek always raises the card; the next point is one tap away on the wall and in Related.
  - **Unity today: Built** (`container.open_stop`, `half_max_ratio` clamped 0.25-0.40, `full_height`, `dismiss_on_tap_outside`,
    `navigation.back_button`). The Lab drew Peek 200 px tall; Unity's 57 pt is the target.
  - **Moving the card takes one gesture.** The grabber (a 36 x 5 pt bar in a 96 x 32 pt target, the iOS / Material sheet handle) is
    a button: a tap moves the card one stop (Peek -> Half -> Full, and Full -> Half), Enter / Space too, named `sheet_expand` /
    `sheet_collapse`. A drag of 40 pt or more moves it one stop its way (down from Peek closes); a shorter one snaps back. A tap on
    the half card's picture (not on a button) and on the peek row raises it one stop too. The first card a visit opens at Half
    shows that it moves, once: it rises 8 pt and settles (0.7 s, after the opening), never with Reduce Motion. **Not yet:** Unity's grabber ignores
    taps (`PickingMode.Ignore`) and a release snaps to the nearest stop by speed (`SheetDrag`, `SheetStopRule.Snap`).
- **6.2 Look.**
  - Default mode: **Light** / Dark / Follow the phone. **Partly:** `ThemeModeRule` has no Follow the phone step.
  - Accent: **Heritage** / Azulejo / Terracotta / Garden. Each colour is measured at 7:1 or more on the surface and the box, light and
    dark (Lab values: light #1B4691 / #13507E / #823114 / #24532F; dark #8FB0EC / #7DB8E8 / #F0A07A / #8CC99B). **Not yet** in the
    Editor (the theme has the four classes).
  - Roundness: Sharp / **Soft** / Round, three radius sets of the theme. PROPOSED (named by the theme, no row in Unity).
  - Layout: **One scroll** / Tabs / Pages / Accordion. Open one at a time: **Off** / On (with Accordion, and for every Expand Row).
    A card with fewer blocks than the bar's Shown from, or with one section, always scrolls. **Built** (`navigation.layout`,
    `accordion_one_open`).
  - Top band: **Fade** / Glass. Fade = the sheet's own colour, solid through the title row (56 pt), faded to nothing by 80 pt.
    There is no Clear: the title would sit on scrolling text. **Built** (`container.top_band`).
  - Swipe strips: **Show the next item's edge** / Whole items only. Every horizontal strip (a peek Carousel, the Timeline strip,
    Related). **Built** as `container.peek_next_card` (`CardTrackRule`).
- **6.3 Sound.** When another sound starts (an audio or a video): **Fade out and switch** (the playing sound fades in 0.5 s and waits
  on its play button) / Lower the other (it goes on quietly under the new one) / Queue the new one (a new audio waits; a video still
  fades the audio out). Never two voices at full volume, so there is no Mix.
  - When earbuds disconnect: **Pause** / Keep playing on the speaker. PROPOSED.
  - **Partly:** Unity has Switch (0.5 s fade) and Queue for audio after audio (`container.audio_when_another_starts`,
    `AudioSwitchRule`, `CardAudioService`), and a fixed rule for audio and video: the one that starts last pauses the other
    (`CardSoundRule`). Lower the other is not built. Unity restarts on the speaker when earbuds disconnect; the Android output poll
    is the framework's own concern, never a wall option.
- **6.4 Mini player.** The audio keeps playing on the wall after the card closes, with Open and Stop. Shown: **On** / Off.
  - With the screen locked: **Pause** / Keep playing. PROPOSED (Unity pauses, `AudioInterruptionRule`).
  - Resume where the visitor left: **On** / Off, within one visit. PROPOSED.
  - **Built** (`MiniPlayerRule`, `container.keep_audio_on_close`).
- **6.5 Media.**
  - Media folder: where the wall's card pictures, sounds and models live. **Built.**
  - Own stock media: optional, the wall's own library, offered first by every media field's Pick default…, then the framework's
    (`CardMediaLibrary`: azulejo pictures, a plain panel, a map plan, a chime, an ambient sound, a tiled 360 room). **Built**
    (`default_media_library_resources_path`, `CardMediaLibraryLookup`).
- **6.6 Saved answers** (Preview & Test): Forget saved answers forgets on this computer everything this wall's cards saved
  (answers, votes, revealed questions, Contents ticks, the picked language). **Built** (Test › Reset Saved Card State).

## 7. Sections

A section is a chapter of the card and one item of the Navigation Bar.

- **7.1 All sections.**
  - Style: **Open** (sections flow on one ground, no frames) / Cards (each section on a glass sheet with framed blocks).
    - With Cards: Glass light **On** / Off (slow coloured light behind each sheet) and Rim light **On** / Off (one rim light on the
      block nearest the middle, never two; the card's one glow, D5). **Built** always on (`CardLight`); the switches are not yet.
    - While Style is Cards, section starts, dividers between sections and tints do not draw; the Editor says so.
  - Block order: **By section** / As placed / As placed, Sources last. **Partly:** Unity has As Authored / End Families Last /
    Grouped By Family (`navigation.block_order`).
- **7.2 Section start:** what marks a section's start.
  - Style: **Tiles** / Wave / Arches / Skyline / Fade (the tint only) / Plain (no edge, no tint) / Own strip picture, plus any edge of
    the Effects pool. There are no Bands: they tinted the whole section.
  - Motion: **Reveal** / Still. Reveal: the tiles turn in one after another, driven by the row's travel, never by a clock; it starts as
    the row clears the bar's resting band and is complete a third of the way up. Replay follows Motion › Replay. Reduce Motion =
    whole. There is no Drift: endless motion beside text (D6).
  - A Tiles or Wave start replaces the divider before it: one ornament per boundary.
  - **Built** (`sections.start` Plain / Bands / Fade / Wave / Tiles, `sections.motion` Still / Reveal / Drift, `SectionStyleRule`);
    Arches, Skyline and the pool edges are not yet. Unity's Plain = the target's Plain; Unity's Fade = Fade.
- **7.3 Divider.**
  - Between sections: **None** / Hairline / Tile Ornament / Tile Row / Mosaic Strip / Own strip picture. Only before a Fade or Plain
    start. **Built** (`sections.divider`, `divider_image`).
  - Between blocks: **None** / any line divider of the pool. **Partly:** Unity always draws a hairline between blocks of an Open
    section (`SectionSheets`).
- **7.4 Section tint:** the colour at a section's top.
  - Colour: **Section colour** / Accent / Neutral (a warm grey tint).
  - Intensity: 0.04-0.20, **0.12**. At 0.30 even white fails 7:1 over a dark tinted start.
  - Tint depth: 40-340 pt, **120**. The tint is a short band, then the paper.
  - The tint is painted as the colour that lands on the sRGB mix the prototype shows (`SectionStyleRule.Tint`). **Built.**
- **7.5 Next Up:** Shown **On** / Off. In the flow at a section's end: the next block's section and name, its place on the card
  ("5 of 13", left out under Pages) and Next. At the card's end: "You have reached the end", no button. Words: `nav_next_up`,
  `nav_next_up_position`, `nav_next_button`, `nav_next_up_done`. **Code kept, not offered** (`NextUpNavView`, `NextUpRule`: a bar at
  the foot); the target is the in-flow row.
- **7.6 Each section** (one foldout per section):
  - Name per language, Icon, Colour (a theme colour).
  - Start: As configured / its own (the 7.2 list).
  - Shown: **On the card and the bar** / On the card only (its blocks stay, no bar item) / Hidden (the section and its blocks leave
    every card).
  - The blocks it holds by default. Each block kind lives in exactly one section. A marker may place one block elsewhere (section 12).
  - Up / Down; Delete only when empty; + Add section; Reset to the framework's sections.
  - The six defaults, in order: About (info, terracotta), Stories (book, wine), Media (image, azulejo blue), Listen (headphones, river
    green), Play (play, brick red), Visit (pin, indigo).
  - Icons: one set, Unity's (`CardIcons.All`, without more, menu and open_block) plus star and ar.
  - A notice names the busiest card when it shows more sections than the bar holds.
- **Three levels, the nearest wins:** card (All sections) -> section -> block.
- **Unity today: Partly.** The section's name, icon, colour, shown and order live in the Navigation Bar's **Bar Groups** table, and
  a group gathers block FAMILIES, not kinds; its Shown only takes it off the bar. To build: Sections as the one owner; the bar reads
  them. **Built rules that stay:**
  - The bar marks the section of the RUN being read (`CardNavModel.Runs`, `NavScrollRule.SectionAt`).
  - A jump lands a section's start fully below the top band (`LandingInset`).
  - Space before a section = twice the block gap. Section openers are not sticky.

## 8. Blocks & Components

Three layers, from small to large:
- **elements** with named styles;
- **patterns** made of elements;
- **blocks**, the things a marker places, with their own options, a preview on the card and an optional full page.

### 8.1 Elements

- **The 24 elements.** Each opens to its named styles, drawn as previews.
  - Text: Title, Eyebrow, Heading, Lede, Paragraph, Callout, Pull Quote, List.
  - Media: Picture, Picture Pair, Carousel, Video, Audio, Animation.
  - Interactive: Before / After, Hotspot Picture, 3D Model, 360 Panorama, Scroll Story.
  - Data: Map, Timeline, Swatch Row.
  - Structure: Divider, Spacer.
  - An app may register its own element kinds (`ElementRegistry`); they appear with their styles.
- **Named styles.** A style is a named set of the element's options. ★ marks the default a new row gets.
  - A style has: Name; the element's options; Entrance (the family's, a motion, or none); for media, With the scroll and By itself;
    for a Carousel shown one at a time, its Transition.
  - Actions: Make default, + Add style (copy), Delete (blocked while used or default). Each shows its use count.
  - A marker's row picks a style; it does not set the options one by one. What stays per row is what depends on the content: the
    words, the media, each item's caption and credit, a focus point and alt text.
- **Options per element** (closed lists; the default first or marked):

  | Element | Options |
  |---|---|
  | Title | Tone, Size (S / M / L), Alignment (Start / Centre) |
  | Eyebrow | Tone, Alignment (written in sentence case, shown in capitals by the language's rules) |
  | Heading | Tone, Size, Alignment, Rule Above |
  | Lede | Size, Alignment |
  | Paragraph | Drop Cap |
  | Callout | Tone, Size, Shape (Box / Postcard, PROPOSED), Interaction (None / Tap to Flip: the Back text) |

### 8.3 The hero (the Header block)

- **Its options** (`bd:Header`):

## 9. Motion & Effects

### 9.1 Motion

- **Motion level** (the wall's): **Full** / Reduced. Reduced gives every visitor whole, still parts. A phone set to Reduce Motion, or
  a visitor who picks Reduced in the Settings panel, always gets Reduced. **Partly:** Unity has the wall switch
  (`container.reduce_motion`) and reads neither the phone's setting nor a visitor's.
- **Motion Family:** the card's default entrance (Lab: Rise). One family per card.
- **Replay** (one choice for the whole card, the section starts follow it): **Every Pass** (scrolling down plays an entrance, up
  undoes it, down again replays) / Once.
- **Reduce Motion, the one hard rule.** Everything is whole and still at once:
  - no reveal, no parallax, no zoom, no pan;
  - demos and shine off;
  - the living glass and the rim light frozen on a lit still frame;
  - the turntable still.
  Other durations are ADVICE (the Editor warns, for example, over 600 ms for an entrance), never caps.
- **Fixed rules:** the stagger stops growing after 5 rows; only the picture nearest the middle moves by itself.
- **Presets by type.** Each preset is named, has its options, a use count and a production path (Native / Build / Import). Delete is
  blocked while used. "+ New" copies a preset of the type.

  | Type | Presets | Options |
  |---|---|---|
  | Entrance | Rise, Slide In, Pop, Fade, Tilt In, Wipe, Build by Words, Build by Letters | comes in from (below / left / right / scale / wipe / in place), distance, tilt, duration, easing (Ease Out / Overshoot / Spring / In-Out), pieces (whole / words / letters), stagger |
  | Transition | Swipe, Cube, Card Deck, Flip, Cross-fade, Zoom Through | turn, duration, easing |
  | With the scroll | Parallax, Zoom with the scroll, Grow In | strength (a stronger look is a copied preset, never a per-row choice) |
  | By itself | Slow Zoom (1 -> 1.06 and back, 28 s), Pan (across its room and back, 26 s) | loop length; a picture or video style picks one |
  | Emphasis | Shine, Pulse, Float | period (loops stop under Reduce Motion) |
  | Imported | Azulejo Draws Itself (Lottie), Caravel Sails In (Rive), Gold Dust (video + alpha), Palace Door Opens (Unity Animation) | source, file, plays (on view / loop / on tap / with scroll) |
- **Unity today: Partly.**
  - `ElementMotion` / `ElementMotionRule`: per-row effects with the prototype's numbers. One driver per composed view, transform and
    opacity only.
    - Enter line 92 %; rise 16; slide 24; 0.36 s ease-out; 60 ms stagger capped at 5.
    - Parallax 0.1x within the frame's 10 % overhang; zoom 1.06 over 28 s; pan half its room over 26 s; strong = 2x.
    - ONE row moves by itself at a time.
  - The Look Kit's Reveal and `KitTicker`.
  - Unity's per-row Motion (Auto / None / Reveal / Slide In / Parallax / Slow Zoom / Pan) and Strength become the style's Entrance,
    With the scroll and By itself; Unity's Reveal = Rise; Auto picks parallax for a picture today.
  - To build: the preset library, the Motion Family, Imported animation, transitions between items, per-piece builds.
- **Production paths:**
  - Native: translate / scale / rotate / opacity per frame; words and letters by per-glyph offsets.
  - Build: Cube, Flip, Tap to Flip. UI Toolkit has no 3D, so these draw the page into a render texture capped near 1.5x, shown on a
    perspective-warped strip mesh.
  - Import: Rive has an official Unity runtime (draws to a texture); Lottie has third-party runtimes only (unverified); video with
    alpha (VP8 / HEVC alpha, per platform, unverified); Unity Animation on a camera into a texture (Native).

### 9.4 Living glass and the rim light (the Cards look)

**Built** (`Runtime/UI/Screen/Cards/Light/CardLight`).

- Reduce Motion = `_Animate` 0, a lit still frame.

  pass every frame.

## 16. Unity 6.3: measured limits

Project: 6000.3.19f1 LTS, URP 17.3.

- **No backdrop blur** (`backdrop-filter` is 6.6+; the Fade top band is a gradient of the sheet colour). **No USS gradients** (6.7):
  use a ramp texture or vertex-coloured bands.
- **USS:**
  - `var()` chains resolve 2-3 deep and are substituted at the USING element (`UssVariableChainTests`).
  - `customStyle` holds only the tokens DECLARED on that element, and a broken `var()` paints transparent with no warning.
  - `cubic-bezier()` in USS imports with a silent warning, then every style update of a matching element throws: use the named
    curves or `experimental.animation` with a custom easing.
  - Never drive one property by both systems.
- **A USS percentage does not resolve inside a ScrollView** (compute fractions in C#). A ScrollView scrolls by `translate`.
- **`filter:`** applies to the element's whole subtree, text included, and costs batches: one focal use only.
- **A material on a parent also draws its children:** an effect is a SIBLING layer behind the content. UI Toolkit does not cull
  clipped elements, so an off-screen material still costs a pass.
- **Shadows:** a 9-slice shadow (generated in code) in light mode; none in dark.
- **Fonts:**
  - One `.ttf` makes a TMP asset and a TextCore asset (one asset cannot serve both; a TMP asset in USS blanks the label).
  - Use static atlases (a dynamic atlas grew 5.8 KB -> 2.1 MB on save).
  - A text effect reaches only as far as the font's SDF padding.
- **Painter2D:** a gradient fill ignores alpha (translucent flat fills use `fillColor`); a 999 px radius is scaled per corner pair
  into an oval.
- **No 3D transforms in UI Toolkit:** a scale stands in, or a render texture on a mesh (9.1).
- **JsonUtility writes every member,** so new values live under reserved keys inside existing classes. Both shipped configs must
  round-trip byte for byte (`ElementDataRoundTripTests`).
- **World-space UI Toolkit exists (6.2+).** Cards "on the wall" need a spike first (the AR camera as event camera, legibility at
  1-3 m, sorting with the markers, cost).
- **Engine upgrade: not now.** 6.7 LTS when it ships, as a trial on a worktree. First pin `com.immersal.core` to a tag or commit.

## 18. Lessons

- Inventory the phone's UI elements first, then attach each option and word to the element that draws it, then audit each word
  against its component. Placing words by key prefix or in parent sections put "Previous section" under Card › Look.
- One list of texts sends the developer looking for where a word shows, and a copy beside the parts drifts. Store each text once, on
  its part, and make every other place a view of it.
- Measure the worst pixel on the render, across the motion (pan, zoom) and with the words hidden; re-measure when layout moves. A
  gradient that looked right measured 1.04:1.
- Shadows vanish in dark mode; a white block's edge over a sunny wall fell to 1.0:1. Choose edges against the worst photo pixel.
- A frame's padding steals width from strips (358 -> 324 px): check track fit when anything adds padding.
- A test's view of the card must match what the visitor sees: the floating bar hid targets that `ScrollTo` left at the viewport's
  edge.
- Check the prototype's own settings before judging it: a motion looked invisible because Reduce Motion was saved on.
- Before a prototype counts as final, walk every Unity Editor row and phone element against it (add, merge, drop with a reason).
  The Lab had invented a "next card peek" from a misread Unity option, and a block's foldout held no option of its kind.
- Measure pixels before and after a behaviour-free refactor (max delta 0); text pixels depend on atlas history, so allow noise.

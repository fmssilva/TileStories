## 1. Decisions

| Topic | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Who Playful is for | **Ages 7–11**, designed for a 9-year-old (core 8–10) | Closest precedents: NG *Keeper of Paintings* AR trail (7–11), Rijksmuseum Family Quest (7+). Children react badly to content even one grade off their age |
| Who Premium is for | **Ages 12+** (teens and adults) | Phone ownership is around 80 % at 11. Teens reject the word "kids" and "babyish" looks |
| The question | "Who is this tour for?" with **Ages 7–11 · playful adventure** and **Ages 12+ · full museum tour**. Note: "Exploring together? Pick the youngest. You can switch anytime." | It names the dimension and starts with the age. It doesn't assume who holds the phone, and it works for mixed families |
| Teens | Premium, plus a later "challenge" layer (quizzes, a shareable tile-poster result card). No third look for now | Cost versus audience; revisit after tests with 12–15-year-olds |
| Illustration source | **The azulejo itself:**<ul><li>cobalt brush line on warm tin-glaze white;</li><li>tile patterns and borders;</li><li>doodles from the panel (ships, coaches, windmill, waves);</li><li>the guide is a **figura de convite** (Azu), a cut-out tile figure</li></ul> | Playful must still read as cultural: "a cultural app, but amazing" |
| Where the fun lives | Hand-drawn frames, stickers, wobble and parallax on **non-camera screens and sheets**. Over the wall, only cobalt rings and pictograms that look like part of the tile; at least 75–80 % of the screen stays the live wall; one motion at a time; celebrations of 1.5 s or less | Attention budget (Apple/Google AR guidelines, split-attention research) |
| Unity | Every technique must map to Unity 6.3 UI Toolkit (see the rules below). `tests/unity_lint.py` enforces them on `css/kids_*.css` | User's rule: "it has to be implementable in Unity" |

## 2. Lab rules (Unity-feasible)

**Do:**
- border-radius, solid borders;
- **border-image 9-slice**, for hand-drawn frames (`-unity-slice-*`);
- sprites and SVG, used as images or inline icons;
- one text-shadow;
- opacity, 2D transforms (translate / rotate / scale);
- transitions with named easings;
- **hard offset shadows**, as a sibling element / pseudo-element, translated (Unity: a sibling element, or the frame sprite tinted ink).

**Do, tagged with the Unity implementation:**
- a soft box-shadow: `/* unity: sprite */`;
- a gradient: `/* unity: ramp */` or `/* unity: sprite */`;
- @keyframes: `/* tween: … */`.

**Don't:**
- backdrop-filter (allowed only for the camera-glass tag, and Playful is solid);
- filter (drop-shadow, brightness, saturate);
- mask / mask-image, mix-blend-mode, clip-path;
- perspective / preserve-3d / rotateX/Y;
- cubic-bezier();
- background-clip:text;
- outline;
- stacked text-shadows, conic-gradient.

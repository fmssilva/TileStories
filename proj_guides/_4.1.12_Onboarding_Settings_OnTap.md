# 4.1.12 · Onboarding v2, Settings and You, the action bar, the immersive card, Coming soon (Lab v20, version 0)

## 1 · Onboarding v2: one page, top to bottom
1. **Pick a look.** Premium and Playful appear as two fixed pictures (in Unity, two sprites), so each card shows its own look whatever look is live. A tap restyles the page at once.
   - Untouched, the look follows the audience (`D.profile.look = "auto"`).
   - Once picked, the look stays.
2. **Quick icons, top right, optional.** Three 48 pt buttons in one capsule: light / dark, language (EN ⇄ PT) and text size (S → M → L). The phone's own settings are the default.
3. **Who is this tour for?** Two plain buttons: Ages 7–11 on the left, Ages 12+ on the right. Under them sits **one** example card: the same POI as each audience gets it.
   - 7–11: Azu speaks, with find-it games, stickers and the guide.
   - 12+: the story at the chosen depth, with guided tours, 3D and 360°, and sources and dates.
   - Note under the card: "Exploring together? Pick the youngest."
4. **How long do you have?** Quick, Classic or Deep, plus "n of N places on the wall".
5. **Anything you love?** Optional interest chips.
6. **Let's go** (sticky) and **Skip**, plus "You can change all of this later in Settings."

**Landscape:** two columns that scroll separately; Let's go stays at the bottom of the right one.

**Editor (App › 3):** ask the look, quick icons, ask who / how long / interests, and the language icon.

## 2 · Settings by area, and You
- **Settings** opens from the **gear** left of the search (Main AR › Search › Settings gear) or from You. It has five areas:

  | Area | Contents |
  |---|---|
  | Your visit | Tour for, Time (with places shown), Interests |
  | Look and reading | Look, Light or dark (Phone / Light / Dark), Text size (Phone / S / M / L), Language |
  | On the wall | Wall mini-map (Auto / Always / Off), Names on the wall, Arrows to places off screen, Highlight my interests, Life on the tiles |
  | Sound and motion | Sounds, Vibration, Reduce motion (Phone / On / Off) |
  | Help and about | See the welcome again, Show the first tips again, Privacy, About |

  - The editor (App › 7) can switch off the last three areas.
  - About 18 controls in all, in short groups. Every option is one tap, and nothing technical is shown.
- **You** is the visitor's own panel:
  - a greeting with their choices as chips (tap one to change it);
  - found / saved / album counts and a progress bar;
  - saved places, with a friendly empty state;
  - the tile album;
  - Settings.
- **Visitor preferences** live in `D.profile.prefs` and are applied at once:
  - mini-map Auto / Always / Off overrides the design default;
  - Names off hides the wall labels, except the selected one;
  - Arrows off hides the pointers;
  - Highlight off removes the interest glow;
  - Life and Reduce motion set the Wall Life motion state.

## 3 · Main AR screen
- **"Lenses" is now "Category filter"** in the editor and in the filter button's label.
- **Action bar · the main buttons** (App › 4) holds 2 to 5 buttons, left to right, from this catalogue:
  - Explore, Azu, You, Tours, Play, Saved, Map, Settings.
  - Map toggles the wall mini-map.
  - Settings is hidden when the gear is shown.

  Azu is raised when it's the mascot. In landscape the bar becomes a rail. In Unity it is one ScriptableObject row per button.

## 4 · On tap
- **Immersive card:**
  - The photo leads, as a child image. Under it come the name and two clamped lines (`shortOf(p)`: Quick's line, or the first sentence at the current profile); the full story is behind **Story**.
  - The action row: Story first, in the accent colour, then icon buttons with one-word labels. They pop in one after another (a tagged tween, off under Reduce motion) and sink when pressed.
  - The spotlight is a soft vignette (34 %, wide) instead of a heavy dim.
  - In landscape the card turns into a row beside the POI.
- **Floating panel:** the same action row as the immersive card. It opens upwards when the POI sits low.
- **"Focus only" is removed:** it left the visitor no way to explore the POI.
- **Coming soon:** for POIs marked on the wall before their story is written.
  - Set it in Specific Marker › Content, or leave the summary and story empty.
  - The name always shows.
  - Every tap style shows the badge and one line. The buttons become **What's coming** and **Save**.
  - The "What's coming" page shows the tiles it will be about, an honest line, and Save for later / Back to the wall.
  - Copy for each audience is in App › 5 › Coming soon:

    | Audience | Copy |
    |---|---|
    | 12+ | "We're still researching this place." |
    | 7–11 | "Azu is still digging up this story!" |

  - Two such POIs are on the wall now: Hilltop convent and Arcaded house.

# Playful look: spec (version 0)

Chosen automatically when the visitor answers "With children". It is the same component set as Premium: only tokens and colours change, plus four new tokens that Premium leaves at their neutral value. Machine-readable values: `playful_spec.json`. Contrast script: `playful/contrast.py`.

**Character.** A polished game UI: solid cream and ink panels, a 2 pt ink "sticker" outline, hard offset shadows that you press "into", rounded Fredoka titles, cobalt (from the azulejo) with sunflower yellow. No mascots, gradients, bevels, drop-caps or clip-art.

## 3. Font

**Fredoka 600**, one weight, from `@fontsource/fredoka/files/fredoka-latin-600-normal.woff2`. The latin subset covers ã, õ and ç.

- **Used for:** titles of 20 pt and up, onboarding headlines, main and secondary button labels (16 pt and up), and numerals in badges.
- **Body stays Atkinson Hyperlegible Next**, confirmed. That covers summaries, chips, list rows and wall labels. Parents read aloud, and early readers need Atkinson's disambiguated glyphs. Fredoka's round terminals blur at 13–15 pt over a busy wall.
- **Why not Baloo 2:** its x-height is taller and its joins heavier, so at 600 it looks bubbly and childish. It also sits less cleanly beside Atkinson.
- **Fallback:** `"Fredoka","Atkinson Hyperlegible Next",system-ui`.

## 4. Treatments (CSS level)

- **Sticker outline.** Use `box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 var(--ol) var(--tb)` with `--ol` = outline_pt. It goes on `.ds-solid`, `.ds-dialog`, `.ds-btn`, `.lchip`, `.ds-seg`, tags, nav and wall labels.
  - Sheets get the outline on the top edge only.
  - List dividers do **not** use the ink. They use `text.soft` at 20%.
  - In dark mode, add an inner rim `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.12)`.
- **Hard shadow.** Use `box-shadow: <outline>, 0 calc(3pt*depth) 0 var(--hard)`.
  - Panels use 4 pt.
  - Anything floating over the camera adds a faint halo `0 6pt 12pt rgba(0,0,0,.18)` so it reads on busy tiles.
  - No soft shadows otherwise.
- **Pressed state.** On `:active`:
  - `translate: 0 2pt`
  - the shadow shrinks to `0 1pt 0`
  - 80 ms down, 220 ms up on the spring
  - with Reduce Motion: no translate, the fill darkens 8% instead
- **Selected chip or segment.** `selected: fill` gives the following:
  - accent fill with accent_text, weight 700
  - a leading check glyph, so status is not shown by colour alone
  - the outline stays
  - the unselected state is the tag surface with the outline
  - the selected tab in the nav uses an accent pill behind the icon plus a bold label
- **Glass.** Solid. No backdrop blur anywhere, which also makes it cheaper on device. The camera never shows through text surfaces.
- **Icons.**
  - Line weight 2.25 on the 24 grid, with round caps and joins.
  - The main button's icon is filled.
  - No multicolour icons. Colour lives in the decor badges, behind an ink glyph.
- **Onboarding cards.**
  - A cream card, 36 pt radius, 2 pt outline and a 4 pt hard shadow, on a sky (#7CC6FF) page in light or #221D45 in dark.
  - At the top, a decor-coloured "tile" vignette (a simplified azulejo motif in ink line on sun or mint).
  - Fredoka 28 pt headline, then Atkinson body at 17 pt.
  - A full-width main button.
  - Progress shown as ink-outlined dots, with the current dot filled and wider.
  - Cards enter with a spring: scale .96 → 1, y 12 pt → 0.
- **Motion.**
  - Durations: 120 / 240 / 380 / 520 ms.
  - Ease: `cubic-bezier(.33,1,.68,1)`.
  - Spring: `cubic-bezier(.34,1.56,.64,1)`; dialogs and sheets use the softer `cubic-bezier(.3,1.3,.5,1)`.
  - With Reduce Motion, transforms, marker pulse and confetti are off, and only 120 ms cross-fades remain.
- **Markers.** Storybook (point_style_6) has a 2 pt white sticker outline and a status badge with a glyph. Its pulse stops under Reduce Motion.

## 5. Do's and don'ts

- **Do** keep one focal fill per view: the accent main button.
- **Do** keep 48 pt targets. Size 1.15 adds visual weight, never smaller hit areas.
- **Do** pair every status with a glyph or word.
- **Do** keep text at 13 pt or larger after the 1.0625 step.
- **Don't** outline text, use gradients, glossy bevels or rotated "wacky" layouts.
- **Don't** put Fredoka below 16 pt.
- **Don't** use decor colours for text, or as the only sign of a state.
- **Don't** put hard shadows on the wall labels' leader lines, or on more than one stacked level. Nested panels sit flat.

## 6. Risks

1. **Visual noise over the wall.** Ink outlines plus Storybook markers plus labels can crowd the blue-on-white panel. Mitigation: wall labels use a 1.5 pt outline and no hard shadow. Test against shots_p6 at 3+ labels.
2. **Dark mode edges.** The ink outline (1.24:1 against the panel) is a depth line, not a boundary. On dark camera frames, panels rely on tone and the inner rim. Check the 3:1 non-text rule on real frames. If it fails, raise the panel to #2D2760.
3. **Cost of the CSS migration.** The 58 dead `.kids` rules and the `:not(.kids)` selectors must go before the `outline_pt`/`shadow_style` variables land. Otherwise the specificity fights will produce half-styled screens. In Unity, `box-shadow` offsets need a 9-slice "shadow plate" sprite, because USS has no hard shadows.

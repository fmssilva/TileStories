Locate Button Study

Three lighter takes on "Show me where it is", drawn on your own card capture. Pick a colour, freeze the motion, or fold the label away as if the visitor had scrolled.

Colour·
A · Floating pinA 3D map pin hovering over the bar, bobbing slowly; its shadow on the bar breathes with it. It says "place" without words.
B · Pin + labelThe same pin with "Find it on the wall" at rest; the words fold away once the visitor scrolls, the pin stays.
C · Glass + pingA light glass disc holding the pin and a soft radar ping every few seconds: the most "AR", still minimal.

What it would do. A tap lowers the card to Peek and, later, draws the way to the marker on the wall in the camera view. A pin is the sign people already read as "where", so the button explains itself; the label in B removes any doubt on first sight.

Words on the Hero

Four ways to keep the hero's words readable on an uneven picture without a dark patch. The words stay put; move the Ribeira Palace picture under them (play, the sliders, or drag) to pass them over stone, roofs, water and bright sky.

or drag any picture
A · Letter haloA tight shadow that hugs each glyph: 1 px close shade + 6 px and 18 px soft glows. No shape on the picture at all.
B · Edge scrimThe picture darkens toward its own foot, on an eased curve (no visible edge). Apple, Netflix, Airbnb.
C · Tone + haloA very wide, very soft darkening anchored at the words' corner, plus a light halo. Reads as lighting, not as a shape.
D · Progressive blurThe picture softens and dims under the words (iOS, Apple Music). Unity has no live blur: a pre-blurred copy of the hero, masked.
Reference · nothingThe words alone, to see what each treatment buys.

How to read it. A is the most invisible but alone is the weakest on bright sky. B is the industry default and the safest for contrast, but the picture itself gets darker at its foot. C is B made local: it reads as light falling off, not as a shape. D is the most premium, and in Unity it needs a pre-blurred copy of the picture.

At a glance

The king’s house on the river

For about 250 years the kings of Portugal lived by the river, in a palace that opened straight onto the Tagus. It is gone now, but the square that took its place still carries its name.

The story · 2 min read

Gold, spices and a view of the fleet

When King Manuel I found the old castle on the hill too cramped for a court growing rich on the sea trade, he moved down to the water’s edge. The new palace stood beside the docks where ships from India and Brazil unloaded spices, sugar and gold.

So the king could watch the fleet come in from his own windows.

Quick question

What stands where the palace stood?

The Praça do Comércio. Rebuilt Lisbon laid out an open square where the palace had stood, facing the river like a grand front door.

Block Components Lab

The Detail Card tab reorganised in seven sections: 0 Preview & Test, 1 Card, 2 Sections (the one place for the card's groups), 3 Blocks & Components, 4 Motion & Effects, 5 Navigation & Controls, 6 Shared Content; then a marker's card and pages. From the smallest part to the whole card: Motion, Interaction and Effects, the Elements with named styles, Patterns made of elements, the Block Library built on them, then a marker's card (its blocks and their previews) and each block's page with its real content fields. Edit anything: the phone jumps to it, marks it EDITING and dims the rest. The floating bar on the phone switches light / dark, motion, language and screen.

In Unity

Elements, stylesNATIVE the components exist; a style is a named set of their classes. BUILD the style tables, previews rendered off-screen into a texture.
Entrances, scroll, emphasisNATIVE translate / scale / rotate / opacity per frame; words and letters by per-glyph offsets (as the Open Row's Shine).
Cube, Flip, Tap to FlipBUILD UI Toolkit has no 3D: the page is drawn into a render texture sized from the panel at the device's scale (recreated on rotation and resize), shown on a perspective-warped strip mesh. Works on any screen; cost grows with resolution, so the texture is capped (e.g. 1.5x).
Imported animationIMPORT Rive: official Unity runtime, draws to a texture. Lottie: third-party runtimes only (unverified). Video with alpha: VideoPlayer (VP8 alpha / HEVC alpha, per platform: unverified). Unity Animation: an animated prefab on a camera into a texture: NATIVE.
InteractionFlip, hold, drag to compare, swipe deck, hotspots, choice: NATIVE pointer manipulators. Scroll Scrub (pinned): BUILD a pinned slot in the stack. Tilt to Look: gyro (Input System attitude) NATIVE.
ScreensOne card, three widths: phone portrait (the sheet), phone landscape (a side sheet over the camera), tablet (a centred reading column; patterns go two-up). Fixed rules per pattern, no per-screen settings.

What to judge

  1. The layers in order: Motion, Interaction, Effects, Elements, Patterns, then Block Library. Easy to find things with the filters?
  2. Imported animation as a motion type (Rive, Lottie, video with alpha, Unity Animation) with a trigger: on view, loop, tap, scrub with the scroll.
  3. The interaction set: flip, hold, compare, swipe deck, scroll scrub, tilt, choice, hotspots. Missing one?
  4. Edges and line dividers as effects, and the Divider element picking one. Enough shapes?
  5. Specific Marker in two parts: the card (blocks, previews, what a tap does) and the pages (content fields). Right split?
  6. The phone's floating bar (theme, motion, language, focus, screen) and the Editor's foldout options at the top.

Values in this Lab are experiments, not defaults: defaults are chosen in Unity on the real card.

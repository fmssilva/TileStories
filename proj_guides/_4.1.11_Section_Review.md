# 4.1.11 · Section review: every editor component under every profile (version 0)

The question: does every section and subsection of the editor have a component that looks good under every profile option? The Lab v19 answers it fold by fold.

## Method
- **Inventory:** every fold of the App (46), Global (29) and Specific (18) tabs, with the demo each fold opens (`sections/inventory.py`).
- **Contact sheets:** one per fold (`sections/section_shots.py`).
  - Six profile rows: Premium light, Premium dark, Playful light, Playful dark, Premium text L, Playful text L.
  - Phone portrait and landscape; tablets per group.
- **Six reviewer + fixer groups** worked in parallel. Each owned its files: one Playful CSS, one Premium CSS, plus JS for the wall.
  - For every fold: a verdict per profile, a fix, after-shots, then a confirmed verdict.
  - Changes outside a group's files went in as written proposals. The main session merged them.

## Resulting rules (version 0, also for Unity)
- **One file per group and look.**
  - `kids_00…50_*.css` hold Playful (`.tapc.look-playful`).
  - `prem_{foundations,surfaces,main,tap,flows}.css` hold Premium (`.tapc:not(.look-playful)`).
  - In Unity these become one USS per group per look.
- **Selection is never colour alone.**
  - Premium selected chips: accent ring + check. Selected time card: ✓.
  - Playful: pressed-in sticker + check badge.
- **Pressed and disabled use colour tokens, never filters or opacity.** Disabled: an empty outline, text at 7:1 and a lock.
- **Dark Playful panels are a glazed tile:** an #26306E field with the tile's inner border line (9-slice sprite `KIDS_SURFACES_TPANEL*_D`).
- **The screen card yields to the POI.**
  - Landscape: it tucks to a 48 pt title tab.
  - If the tab still covers the POI, it moves to the top-right corner (`.tuck-top`).
  - Half card ≤ 41 % of the screen.
- **Pointers and labels avoid the UI.**
  - Edge pointers slide out from under the search bar, lenses, bars, rail, corner buttons and mini-map (`clearPointers`).
  - Left-edge pointers put the arrow first (`.at-l`).
  - Wall labels hide under all of those, flip at the right edge, and go above the marker near the bottom.
  - Wall labels follow text size, with a 13 pt floor.
- **Every target is ≥ 48 pt.** That includes edge pointers, the pin, the avatar, × buttons, 3D chips and floating panel links.
- **Wall demos never show the room past the wall** (`runDemo` → `camCover`).
- **Premium has no mascot or confetti.** The sticker moment becomes a quiet "Added to your album" toast; the album is a calm collection.
- **Playful pins its accent;** the accent list and editor row are hidden there.
- **The immersive card's photo is a child image,** not the card's background.

## Final pass: independent critics, then two fixers
- **Critics:** two critics who had not done the work read the 46 final App sheets, one for Premium and one for Playful.
- **Dismissed:**
  - The photo credit is baked into the wall photo, and the physical wall has none.
  - Section headers with no demo were a capture problem, now fixed: a section header shows its own screen.
- **F-tap fixed:**
  - The tap demos were finishing after the shot. Camera and finger now run together, and the tap fires at 380 ms.
  - The demo camera keeps the wall over the whole screen.
  - Labels and pointers avoid every piece of tap UI, and labels are placed by measurement (own side → other side → below → slide inside).
  - Tags avoid the whole marker.
  - The radial ring opens up or down in landscape.
  - The "On the wall" row sits below the Buttons panel.
- **F-kit fixed:**
  - Kit pages in phone landscape use two columns, most important first.
  - Pages end with an 84 pt fade plus a "More" button (Unity has no mask).
  - The Overview nav is pinned.
  - Sheets: buttons pinned below the scrolling body; in landscape a full-height sheet on the right.
- **Minor, kept:**
  - In landscape, tapL_5, tapL_6 and actc_5 tuck the card to a title pill when the POI is under it. This is by design.
  - Playful text L: "3D viewer" wraps to its own row.

# App Design System: final tokens and configs (4.1.7)

Version 0. This is the single place for the app-wide design tokens: the AR screens, On tap, Start, Onboarding, Explore and every
app page. The POI detail card keeps its own guide (`_3.3`); this guide **adopts the card's Unity theme as the base** and adds
only what the app screens need. The World Editor Lab's App › 1 Design system edits exactly these tokens and shows each on the
phone.

## 1. Sources and decisions

- **Base:** the Unity theme, `Theme.default.asset` → `Runtime/UI/Theme/Generated/Theme.default.uss` (`--ts-*`), and the fixed
  rules of `_3.3` section 1 (D1-D8). The Lab now uses the same values and the same font files.
- **Research** (2025-26: Apple HIG Materials and AR, Material 3 Expressive, ARCore UI, DTCG 2025.10) and a token-by-token
  reconciliation (`ds_review/token_reconciliation.md`, contrast measured in linear light like Unity's own tests).
- **Rule of change:** keep the Unity value unless it breaks a fixed rule or the app screens need something it lacks.

## 2. Fixed rules (never an option)

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Text contrast | 7:1 on its ground, both modes; 4.5:1 for words on a photo at the worst pixel; icons and edges 4.5:1 / 3:1 |
| Ink | solid colours only; no alpha on text |
| Minimum text | 13 pt |
| Touch target | 48 × 48 pt, 8 pt between two |
| Grid | every size on the 4 / 8 pt scale; every value a token |
| One focal action | at most one accent-filled action in view (D5) |
| Accent | a closed list, each measured; no free accent colour |
| Reduce Motion | the phone's setting always wins: whole and still, a 120 ms cross-fade at most |
| Safe areas | nothing under the status bar, the island or the home bar |
| AR | primary controls in the bottom third; coaching hides unrelated UI; no full-screen takeover over the camera |

**Accent** (closed list, Unity's four classes; the main button, selected chips, the nav disc, switches). Accent-coloured TEXT
sits only on solid surfaces; on glass and over the camera, text buttons are ink (underlined).

| Accent | Light / on it | Dark / on it |
|---|---|---|
| **Heritage** (default) | #1E3A8A / #FFFFFF | #F2B544 / #1A1206 |
| Azulejo | #13507E / #FFFFFF | #7DB8E8 / #0B1A26 |
| Terracotta | #823114 / #FFFFFF | #F0A07A / #2A0F05 |
| Garden | #24532F / #FFFFFF | #8CC99B / #0C2213 |

**Mode:** **Light** (default, sunlight first) / Follow the phone / Always dark.

**Glass rendering** (Unity 6.3 has no backdrop blur): **Blurred camera** (default: once per frame, the AR camera image is
blurred into a 1/8 render texture, its luminance shifted toward the glass tone, and used as the glass part's background under
the tint) / **Solid** (the tint alone at 0.84 light / 0.98 dark; also the Reduce Transparency fallback). The blur's tints are
valid only with the luminance limit, which the shader must hold and a test must measure: the blurred backdrop at most 0.10
linear in dark and at least 0.25 in light (then ink keeps 7:1 at 0.80 / 0.78). Until that shader is built and measured, Unity
ships Solid; the Lab shows Blur as the target design.

### 3.3 Space and shape (KEEP grid; NEW roundness sets)

Spacing 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48. **Density** Comfortable / Compact (inner paddings one step down; targets stay 48),
set by the audience (Expert = Compact), not a global setting.

| Roundness | small | medium | cards, panels | sheets, dialogs | buttons, chips |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sharp | 4 | 8 | 12 | 16 | 8 |
| **Soft** (default, = Unity 6 / 12 / 20 / 30) | 6 | 12 | 20 | 30 | pill |
| Round | 8 | 16 | 24 | 32 | pill |

**Icon line** 1.5 px (default) / 2 px on the 24 pt grid (Family 2).

### 3.4 Depth and motion

- **Elevation:** light = three shadow levels (floating 0.20, raised 0.32, overlay 0.40); dark = no shadows on panels (surface
  tone + a 1 px rim); anything floating over the camera keeps a soft shadow in both modes. Config: **Shadows** Off / Standard /
  Strong.
- **Durations:** 120 (a tap answers) / 220 (panels, chips) / 360 (sheets, push) / 500 ms (the camera). Exits ≈ 0.75×.
- **Easing:** ease-out-cubic; **Motion style** Standard (no overshoot, default) / Expressive (springs that overshoot a little;
  Family). Unity: springs from C# (USS cubic-bezier throws in 6.3).
- **Motion:** Follow the phone (default) / Always full / Always reduced.

### 3.5 Audiences (`_3.5` §3.5: token deltas on one component set)

| | Explorer (default) | Family | Expert |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corners | Soft | Round | Sharp |
| Text | ×1 | one step larger (body 17, ×1.0625) | ×1 |
| Motion style | Standard | Expressive | Standard |
| Density | Comfortable | Comfortable | Compact |
| Icon line | 1.5 | 2 | 1.5 |

The old cartoon "Family" skin (Baloo 2, thick outlines, orange text at 2.2:1) is retired.

## 5. Fixed in this pass, by rule

- The Start screen's hero is always the dark scheme (the wall under a warm shade), so its main button is the dark accent in
  both modes.
- Coaching hides unrelated labels; every screen over the camera has a soft top shade so the status bar reads.
- Free colour editing stays in the Lab (Dark / Light design folds) for prototyping, with live 7:1 badges; Unity keeps the
  closed lists.

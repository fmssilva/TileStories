# 4.1.8 · Markers and Regions: Design (version 0)

TileStories · World Editor Lab v17 · Global Scene › Looks › Point styles and Region styles.

This guide records the defaults chosen in the v17 review: what Unity already had, the research, two design critics, and the config audit. The Lab's `config.json` preview carries the same values.

## 1. What Unity already has

- **Markers.** `marker_shape` can be circle, rounded_square, hexagon, diamond, star or none. Each marker gets a category colour and an icon from the icon library. The icons are photoreal, game-like PNGs (rubble with fire, gear and coin, bridge…).
- **Status outline ring.** Its dash rhythm shows the status: intact is solid, partial is long dashes, destroyed is short dashes, unknown is dotted. It is one colour, #E3BD72, at ring_size_ratio 1.18.
- **Badge.** Ratio 0.36, top right, off by default.
- **Icon and label.** Icon ratio 0.56. Labels are Oswald Bold.
- **Per-level settings.** Size in cm (30 / 20 / …), effects (ripple discs, halo ring, pulse, rotating contour), reveal delays, plus LOD, displacement and orientation.
- **Six categories:** Royal #FFC786, Religious #CB80FF, Military #B44848, Residential #FF9FFC, Economic #8F5E48, Infrastructure #9DDEFF.
- **Regions:** not built in Unity yet (the _4.1 notes only). The Lab is the reference.

## 2. Principles (fixed rules, not options)

1. **The wall never changes.** The camera always sees cobalt on white tiles, in light design and in dark. Only the app chrome follows light / dark.
   - Wall graphics therefore use one **wall ink**: gold #E3BD72, Unity's ring colour, the one ink that reads on azulejo blue.
   - Effects use #F2CA71.
   - A cobalt line disappears into the tiles.
2. **Depth on azulejo comes from shadow plus light.**
   - A gold glow alone has no contrast on cream tiles. Each line is drawn as a dark contact shadow (#08101C, about 1.2 cm blur, 0.25 cm drop), then a glow added with light, then the line.
   - Markers carry a contact ellipse under them, a glass top highlight, and a soft drop shadow.
3. **Never "a polygon".** Outlines get rounded corners (each real corner gets an arc of corner smoothing × 6 cm, at most 45 % of the shorter edge; 0.5 by default) and drawn thin (0.4 cm). Forgiving styles (brackets, wash, inner light, spotlight) don't depend on an exact outline at all.
4. **Status is never shown by colour alone.**
   - The ring's dash rhythm carries the status.
   - Badges carry a distinct mark: ✓ intact, half disc for partial, × destroyed, ? unknown.
5. **Accessibility.**
   - Text is 13 pt minimum. Labels are 14 pt (15 pt in the display face on the Medallion).
   - The tap area is 48 pt whatever the marker size.
   - Reduce Motion freezes effects and turns intros into fades.
   - No more than two things animate at once.
6. **Focus = lift, not more rings.**
   - A tapped marker grows 14 % and its shadow deepens and drops.
   - A tapped region brightens (×2), its wash fades up, and a spotlight dims the rest (0.55).
7. **Ranges are sliders.** Every size, width, strength, opacity, duration and the level reveal delay is a slider. Choices that are categories stay as selects. In the App domain, corners, density, icon line and immersive tag width are sliders too.
8. **Size.** Markers keep a constant size on screen (pt, clamped by the 48 pt tap area), which is the modern AR standard. Unity's per-level size_cm becomes each level's point style size.

## 3. Point styles (presets)

| Preset | Look | Default for |
|---|---|---|
| **Glass chip** | 32 pt glass disc, category-coloured line icon, 1 pt white rim, status ring, glass chip label at right | Landmark, and the app default |
| **Pin dot** | 16 pt category dot, white rim plus dark hairline, halo label 13 pt, no status ring | Detail |
| **Medallion** | 44 pt panel-colour disc, 1.5 pt light rim, gold halo (0.8), label below in Fraunces 15 pt | Hub |
| **Tile** | 36 pt rounded square (echoes the azulejo), category icon, a status ring that follows the shape | Alternative |
| **Storybook (Family)** | The project's photoreal symbols with a real 2 pt white sticker outline, pulse, and a badge whose glyph shows the status | Family audience |

- **Dropped:** Beacon as a preset. It is now an *effect* any style can use, for example on a featured Medallion.
- **Effects:** none, pulse, halo, ripple, beacon. On the phone they animate in one shared loop.
- **Base values:**

  | Setting | Default |
  |---|---|
  | size | 32 pt |
  | category shown as | icon colour |
  | icon ratio | 0.56 |
  | icon line | 1.6 |
  | rim | 1 pt light |
  | shadow | 0.5 |
  | label | chip, 14 pt, right |
  | status | ring at max(1.18 × r, r + 3 pt) |
  | badge ratio | 0.36 |
  | intro | pop |

- **Labels** are measured from the marker's centre: past its radius, its ring and a 6 pt gap. A label never covers its own marker.

## 4. Region styles (presets)

**Base values (the "Soft glow" defaults):**

| Setting | Default |
|---|---|
| Line width | 0.4 cm, clamped to 1.2–4 px |
| Corner smoothing | 0.5 |
| Colour | wall ink |
| Glow | 2 cm at 0.7, with the contact shadow |
| Fill | tint 0.18, feathered 2 cm |
| Intro | draw on, 0.9 s |
| Idle | breathe, 4 s at 0.35 (line, glow and wash) |
| Focus | spotlight 0.55 in 0.36 s |

| Preset | What it is | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Soft glow** | The base above | Hub, Landmark |
| **Hairline** | 0.3 cm, glow 0.4, no fill, focus brighten ×2 | Detail lines, cornices |
| **Focus brackets** | Four corner brackets around the region's box (margin 2 cm). They close in on tap | Precise-free highlight, the most premium |
| **Route** | 0.35 cm solid at 0.45, two comets running along it, drawn on in 1.2 s | Paths, streets, rivers |
| **Wash** | No line; a radial #FFF1D0 wash, blended "screen" so the tiles glow | Areas, soft emphasis |
| **Media window** | The shape shows a picture or video (cover, 1 cm feather) under a faint glow line; video plays on tap | Windows, doors |
| **Quiet (appears on tap)** | Nothing at rest; points show as small rings on tap | Secondary shapes |
| **Spotlight (one hero)** | Dims the wall around the region (0.3, light ×0.7), feather 8 cm | One hero POI per view |
| **Inner light** | No line; a warm screen-blended inner glow that breathes | The most forgiving when tracking drifts |

**Editor (Region style):**
- Line: width, min / max, **corner smoothing**, colour (wall ink / category / fixed), opacity, pattern (solid / dashed / dotted / **focus brackets**), offset (bracket margin), glow, double line.
- Vertices.
- Fill: none / tint / gradient / **spotlight** (dim) / pattern / media, with **blend** (normal / screen) and feather.
- Motion: intro, idle and focus.
- Every range is a slider.

**Point style demo:** opening Point styles previews the selected style on every POI of the wall, framed whole.

**Style demo:**
- Picks the shapes that use the style, or Castle hill when none do.
- Frames one POI at a time, the most central one, fitted to the screen.
- Loops intro → idle → focus → back.

## 5. Open decisions for Unity

- A spotlight fill on several shapes of one style darkens twice. Keep the Spotlight preset for one hero per view (one mask per frame is the fix if needed).
- On the wall, a label that would leave the panel should flip side. This is done on the phone, still to do in the wall view.
- Port the marker drawing (one routine for the wall and the phone) to a UI Toolkit custom mesh or a URP sprite shader.

# 3.8 Guidance: when and how the guide speaks up

One system decides when the guide takes the initiative: the app's tips, remarks on what the camera sees, play, and care. It
merges two independent plans, each researched separately (`_3.8_guidance_plans/plan_A.md` and `plan_B.md`: the research, with
every source linked, and the long tables). This file holds the merged design; the plans are its references.

Status: **decided** (the developer, 2026-11-06: section 9); built round by round (section 8).

## 1. The answer in short

1. **Yes, two big sets (and two more), under one brain.** Today the wall pill, the card Hints, the ✦ hint, Azu's remarks and
   the Moments each run their own clock and can collide. One scheduler, one at a time, one memory.
2. **Push and pull.** The kinds below are *push* (the guide starts). What the visitor asks for (the ✦ menu, Ask, "Things to
   try", "How does this work?") is *pull*: never scheduled, never capped, always one tap away.
3. **The kinds:** **App** (how to use it), **Content** (what is here, what next), **Fun** (play), and **Care** (look up from
   the phone, a break, glare, battery: a group under App, started by events, outside the mix). System states (wall not
   found, offline, loading) are not guidance: while one shows, all guidance waits.
4. **Teach the app through the content.** The best tip is a content line that teaches a gesture: "That's Belém Tower. Tap it
   and I'll show you around." Each item says which feature it **Teaches**; one tap closes both.
5. **Gone once learned** (Apple TipKit's rule): an app tip retires for good once its feature is used. So app guidance fades by
   itself; the 70 % → 15 % mix along the visit is a weight on top, never a quota.
6. **Rare, calm, precise is premium:** one bubble, one ring, one mascot gesture, one short line (12 words or fewer), at a pause,
   beside the thing it is about, and long silences.

## 2. What the research says (details and sources in the plans)

- **Platforms:** Apple's TipKit (eligibility rules, display frequency, `MaxDisplayCount`, invalidation once the action is done,
  ordered tip groups); Material's feature discovery (one at a time, at the moment of need, a dismissal waits longer); Apple's
  onboarding guidance (ask for a permission when it is needed, keep onboarding short and skippable).
- **NN/g:** contextual help beats up-front tutorials, which are skipped and forgotten; coach marks are ignored when they come
  in a row; pull help must stay findable.
- **Product-tour tools** (Appcues, Pendo, WalkMe, Intercom, Userpilot): objects (tour, tooltip, hotspot, checklist, launcher),
  global throttling (a gap between any two, delays not drops), priorities with an "urgent" override, frequency modes (once,
  until done, every time with a cooldown); a manual launch ignores the limits.
- **Mascots and games:** Duolingo (a character with personality, novelty and recovering rewards, never the same line twice),
  Headspace (calm, few words), Pokémon GO's professor (onboarding by a character, then silence), game tutorials and Navi /
  Clippy (the lesson: interrupting at the wrong moment and repeating kills goodwill).
- **Interruptions research** (Adamczyk & Bailey, Horvitz): speak at breakpoints in the task, lower the cost of a wrong guess,
  learn from what the visitor ignores.
- **Museum and city guides** (Smartify, Bloomberg Connects, audio guides, AR city guides): the visitor is on their feet and
  looking at the object; prompts are about what is in front of them; offers of a tour are invitations, not interruptions.

**First principles for a wall:** the visitor stands outdoors, phone raised, arms tiring after 20-40 s, often once only. The wall
is the point, not the phone. Needs come in order: is it working → what is here → how do I get more → tell me → let me look in
peace → play → take care of me.

## 3. The model

### 3.1 Hierarchy

`Overall → Kind → Group → Item`. Each level has the same timing and look fields; a level below inherits them and the Editor
shows the inherited value in words ("As the kind (Ring)"), as it already does for blocks. A **Group** is All (each item on its
own) / One of these (variants: the least seen shows) / In order (a sequence: How to use the card, the first pages).

Default library (abridged; full lists in the plans):

```
App      The start (In order)   Opening message (Essential) · Tap a marker (teaches Open a card) · Point at the tiles
         The wall               Ask about the view · Move closer · The ✦ ("Need anything? Tap ✦")
         The card (cues)        the six card Hints (grabber, Locate label, Navigation Bar, Before / After, strips, underlined words)
         The guide              Pause any time · Hide the dock · Talk or type · Stop and resume
         How to use the card    (In order, pulled only)
         Care                   Lower the phone · Look up near the street · Strong sun: dark mode · Battery low
Content  What's in view         {point} in the middle · {point} has a card: tap and I'll show you around   (one per wall point)
         Tours                  A tour of {point}, {min} min? · Resume your tour
         Routes and finds       A route of this wall · The oldest here · Another card nearby
         This point's own       (per point, Specific Marker > Guide script) "Find the crack from 1755, bottom left"
Fun      Moments                Water break · Cookie (between tour steps)
         Play                   A joke · Treasure hunt? (Family) · Seasonal
```

Under each item, a read-back line in words: *"Tap a marker · App › The start · Essential · when a marker is in view and the
phone is still · at most about every 25 s, up to 3 times a visit, until the visitor opens a card."* Named warnings catch items
that can never show, lines too long, and duplicated lines.

**Events** (the same names in the Lab and in Unity, shown in the Editor by their words): visit started, wall found / lost, a
marker in view, a point settled, phone moving / still, card opened, card at Peek / Half / Full, page opened, block in view,
tour started / step ended / ended / stopped, ✦ menu opened, Ask opened, talk held, Ask about the view opened, Navigation Bar
used, Locate used, Before / After dragged, strip swiped, term opened, lens chosen, Settings opened, audience set, mascot poked,
idle, and the guidance's own shown / dismissed / acted.

### 3.3 The scheduler

A pure function (testable with a fake clock): `pick(items, visitor, context, now) → {item, why} | {none, why}`, checked 4 times
a second.

1. **Gates (fixed, no option breaks them):** one at a time; never over a sentence (any voice) or the visitor speaking; quiet in
   a tour except items allowed between steps; quiet while the visitor acts (a touch in the last 0.8 s, a drag, the phone
   moving in the last 0.7 s, the card's first 1.5 s, a panel open: Settings, Ask, the ✦ menu, the dock at Full); quiet while a
   system state shows; never over its own target or the block being told. Pulled items pass the limits, never the gates.
2. **Limits (Overall rows):** breathing room 15 s between any two (from the end of one), at most 2 a minute, at most 30 a
   visit; each kind's own cap and gap; each item's Shows and Again after.
3. **The stage of the visit:** `s = max(active minutes / 15, share of the milestones done)` (a card opened, a tour step heard,
   a question asked, Ask about the view used, a page opened). A returning visitor starts later (5 min). So a visitor who learns
   fast hears content sooner, and one who ignores tips hears something else.
4. **Score** of each eligible item: `importance × along-the-visit(s) × kind weight(s) × group weight × freshness × fit`.
   Kind weights at s = 0 / 0.3 / 1: **App 70 / 40 / 15, Content 25 / 50 / 60, Fun 5 / 10 / 25** (to judge in the real scene).
   Freshness recovers since the item last showed (`1 − e^(−t/τ)`); fit is ×1.5 when the target is in the middle, ×1.3 when
   the item Teaches an open app tip. Ties: the Editor's order. Deterministic, so "Why not now?" can always answer.
5. **Learning from the visitor:** three ignored in a row → the breathing room doubles (up to ×4) for the visit; an acted item
   resets it. A long press on a bubble → "Fewer tips like this?" (the group rests for the visit). A dismissed App tip waits
   ×3; a dismissed Content or Fun item rests its group 5 min.
6. **Talkativeness** (one knob over all the numbers): Quiet / **Balanced** / Chatty. A typical first visit: about 10 items in
   15 minutes, at most 2 a minute, none over a sentence.

### 3.4 The visitor's controls and memory

- On a bubble: a tap does its action; a swipe or ignoring it dismisses; a long press: "Fewer like this?".
- Settings > The guide > **Tips**: All / Essentials only / Off ("You can always tap ✦ for help"); **Tips stay until I tap
  them** (WCAG 2.2.1); **Show the tips again** (resets the memory, with an Undo toast).
- The ✦ menu gains **Things to try** (the App items as a checklist, ticked when done, each with Show me) and **How does this
  work?** (the walkthrough). Ask Azu answers "how do I…?" from the same items.
- Remembered on the phone only, never sent: per item (shown this visit / ever, last shown, the line used, done and by what,
  dismissed, acted); per visitor (visits, active minutes, the Tips setting, met the guide, the features used). A visit ends
  after 30 min away. **Remember across visits** On / Off (Off for kiosks and demos).

## 4. Presentation (each with its Unity path)

- **Bubble:** opaque paper like the dock, 7:1 text, at most 2 lines, a tail to the mascot; in 220 ms (scale 0.94 → 1 and fade),
  out 160 ms; the spoken word lit. Placed beside its target on the side with room, never over the target or the words being
  read. Unity: a 9-slice `VisualElement`, USS transitions on scale and opacity, a small placement solver on `worldBound`.
- **The mascot moves to the thing:** a short arc hop (0.45 s) to the bubble's side, then points (the point clip); on the wall
  it walks to the point. Unity: a translate tween; the 3D mascot's render texture with the Animator's point clip.
- **Focus:** Glint (the element shines once), Ring (one soft pulse, then still), Spotlight (the rest dims, the target cut out:
  Painter2D `FillRule.OddEven`, as the guide's focus already does). Spotlight is kept for Essentials and walkthroughs, or it
  loses its force.
- **Ghost finger** for gestures words cannot carry (Before / After, swipe strips).
- **Reduce Motion:** no hop (the mascot appears there), no glint sweep, a still ring; the words stay.
- **Accessibility:** the bubble is announced politely to the screen reader and stays until dismissed; no spoken tip over
  VoiceOver / TalkBack; every tip also lands in the dock's conversation under "Tips", so a bubble is never the only channel.
- **Mascot Off:** a plain bubble at the target, no character.

## 6. Onboarding at the app's start: the same system

The first pages are an **In order** group with the **Page** form; the scheduler is quiet while it runs. Under 30 s, every page
skippable:

1. **Hello:** the mascot forms from a tile and waves: "Olá! I'm your guide to Lisbon's tiles." The language as a chip; **Let's
   go** / **Show me how it works**.
2. **Who's exploring** (if Where to ask = At the app's start; Skip = Explorer): it sets the audience, and with it the guidance
   preset.
3. **The camera**, asked when needed: "I need the camera to see the tiles. Nothing is recorded." Then the system prompt; if
   refused, how to allow it, with a button.
4. **Straight to the wall:** the short Opening message ("Point at the tiles and tap a glowing marker"), then Tap a marker.

No carousel of benefits, no account, no tutorial of the card before a card exists. Later launches: no pages, a "Welcome
back". The Lab's preview bar gets **Start the app** to replay the first run.

## 9. Decisions (the developer, 2026-11-06)

1. **The Editor's home:** a top-level **Guide** tab holding today's 6 · Guide, Guidance and the first pages (A).
2. **Spoken tips:** App and Fun as a bubble only; Content spoken once the visitor has heard the guide this visit.
3. **The mix:** a weight in a deterministic score, so "Why not now?" always has an answer.
4. **Card Hints in the Editor:** edited in Guidance, with a view in Card Controls ("Edited in Guidance").
5. Still to judge in the real scene, with five visitors on a wall: the mix numbers, the 15 s breathing room, about 10 tips in
   15 minutes, and whether Care tips feel caring or nagging.
6. **Entertaining the tour (2026-11-10):** a third dimension beside teaching the app and telling the content: what real
   guides do to keep a tour alive (the research: `_3.8_guidance_plans/research_tour_entertainment.md`). It lives in a new group,
   **Fun › On the tour**, with its own rhythm (about one beat per 75 s of narration, at most 3 a tour); the water and cookie
   moments move into it. The first build: the promised quiz, Don't say it!, Find it on the wall, Guess the year, and the
   breather ending with a choice. A game **starts** (no asking first), with a 4 s "Not now" chip; after two "Not now"s, no
   more games this visit.
   The quiz questions and the forbidden words: the Editor drafts them from the tour's own text, and each needs the author's
   **Reviewed** tick before it plays. Answers by tap always, and by voice as an extra where the mic is allowed.
7. **The guide panel (2026-11-10):** four looks built side by side so the developer can choose which options to keep.
8. **The music bed (2026-11-10):** free-licensed tracks offered as "feelings" to the visitor too (Exploration by default);
   four CC0 tracks and one real 16th-century Iberian recording (Cabezón, played by Joan Benson, CC BY-SA 2.0, credited in
   the app). A free real recording of La Folía was not found; the only free one is a synthetic MIDI.

# World Design (4.1.5) -- the feature map, the App screens and the On-tap system

**What this file is.** Every feature that can represent or enrich a POI on the wall, every wall-wide experience, and where
each lives in the POI Editor. It covers the App tab in full (the screens around the wall, Pointing and the On-tap system).
The Editor rules and the Region spec are in `_4.1.3`; Wall Life in `_4.1.4`. The World Editor Lab (`_4.1.2_Prototype/`)
shows all of it working.

---

## 1. The model: three scopes, four moments

**Scopes:** **Wall** (a feature over many POIs: a tour, a game, a map overlay, birds over the roofs) · **POI** (what one
POI shows and offers) · **Screen** (UI over the camera: the card sheet, the guide, viewers).

| Moment of a POI | Question | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **At rest** | How do I see that something is here? | layers: Point, Region, Tag (from a plain label to a card), Model; media in a region |
| **Alive** | Does it move on its own? | Wall Life rows K1-K6 (+ ambient sound) |
| **On tap** | What happens when I touch it? | focus, tag, action buttons, screen card, ask field (section 3) |
| **Opened** | What can I do with it now? | the actions (section 3.1) |

Per POI, **Presentation** groups At rest → On tap → Alive. Wall Life rows that belong to no POI (birds, the mascot) stay
wall-level.

---

## 2. What represents a POI at rest (layers)

A level switches each layer on or off (P point · R region · T tag · M model); a POI may override. Each layer has named
styles in Global Scene › Looks (define, assign, place).

| Layer | What the visitor sees | Unity | Precision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Point** | today's marker: shape, icon, badge, ring, effects | uGUI world-space (exists) | low |
| **Region** | outlines and fills on the wall (+ media fill) | spline mesh + shader | medium-high |
| **Tag** | from a plain label (one title block) to a small card (image, title, one line); tap opens the real card | UI Toolkit, built from Detail Card blocks, anchored to the POI | low |
| **Model** | a 3D model standing on or out of the wall | prefab under the wall root + wall occluder | medium |

A Tag shows only when space allows (Displacement / LOD decide, as for labels). Model at rest is rare; the common case is
3D on tap.

## 3. On tap

**A tap never moves the camera.** On the device we cannot move the visitor's phone, so the tap UI opens around the POI
where it is on the screen, and follows it as the phone moves.

### 3.1 The actions (framework code)

| Action | What it does | Content the POI supplies | Shows when ("auto") |
|---|---|---|---|
| Card | opens the Detail Card sheet | card content | always |
| 3D on the wall | the model rises out of the wall, in place; rotate, zoom, move, moments | model (`POIs/<id>/models/`) | the POI has a 3D model |
| 3D on screen | a viewer over the camera | the same model | the POI has a 3D model |
| 360 visit | an equirectangular photo or video around you | pano (`POIs/<id>/pano/`) | the POI has a 360 photo |
| Then & now | today's photo over the tiles, a wipe | a today photo + its 4 corners on the wall | the POI has a today photo |
| Play | a video or audio clip | media | the POI has a video or audio |
| Today on the map | where it is today: pin, directions | lat / lon (geo-reference) | -- |
| Ask the guide | Azu answers about this POI | -- | -- |
| Navigate here · Favourite · Photo & share · Start a tour here | | -- | -- |
| Quiz | this POI's questions | questions | the POI has a quiz |

Each action type knows its controls ("3D model" knows rotate, zoom, move and **play moment**). The POI fills the content
in Specific Marker › Content for actions: the model file, which controls the visitor may use, and the **moments** (named
clips inside the prefab: "Rebuild the church", "1755: the earthquake"). The same action has two presentations that read
the same content: on the wall (App › On tap) and on screen (Detail Card blocks).

3D formats: any format at authoring time (glTF / GLB, FBX, OBJ, Blender, USD) is imported into a prefab under
`POIs/<id>/models/`; at runtime the app loads the prefab or a GLB streamed with glTFast.

### 3.2 The On-tap system: three layers (App › 5 On tap)

```
5 On tap
   Default style for the whole app: [Radial ▾]
   > Base elements        the buttons and how a button looks
   > Tag components       finished tags
   > Action components    finished button groups
   > On-tap styles        the compositions
   When the phone moves   screen card makes way · keep inside the screen
```

Each subsection previews on the device when it is opened or edited: Base elements shows every button in a gallery; a tag
or an action component shows alone on the POI; a style plays a tap.

**1. Base elements.** One line per action type, in priority order (the first ones show, the rest go under "more"):
label · shows: *When the POI has …* / *Always* / *Off*. **Button look**, shared by every action component: size, shape
(circle / rounded square), surface (glass / solid light). Icons are framework assets.

**2. Components.**

| Tag component | Kinds | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Label, Chip, Compact card, Immersive card, Floating panel | `label` / `chip` / `card` / `immersive` / `panel` | name; size S / M / L (immersive); picture photo / video (loops, muted) / none (card, immersive, panel); category; one-line summary |

| Action component | Kinds | Settings |
|---|---|---|
| Radial ring, Capsule, Links in the tag, Dock, Tiles | `radial` (around the POI) / `capsule` (floating) / `in_tag` / `dock` (a row at the bottom, scrolls sideways) / `tiles` (in the screen card) | name; at most N buttons; labels under the icons |

The pictures are the POI's hero photo or clip. The Floating panel has a glass backing (blur, gradient, soft shadow) so its
text reads on busy tiles.

**3. On-tap styles.** A style puts components together:

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Focus | ring / pulse / spotlight (dims the rest of the screen) / none |
| Tag | a tag component or none; placement auto / above / below |
| Actions | an action component or none |
| **Distance to the marker** | phone units, 0-20: how far the tag and the buttons sit from the marker (bigger for a big marker or a 3D model) |
| Screen or wall | Focus / Tag / Actions on the wall (each on / off); **distance from the wall** (cm, 0-40) |
| On the screen | screen card none / peek / half; ask field off / in the capsule / in the screen card |

**Screen or wall.** A part on the screen is drawn next to the POI and follows it. A part on the wall is placed once, when
the POI is tapped, then stays in the world: the focus is painted on the tiles; the tag and the buttons float at the
distance from the wall, joined to the marker by a thin needle, with parallax when the visitor walks to the side. The
marker never moves. The screen card and the ask field are always on the screen.

### 3.3 Assignment and overrides

- **App default** (App › On tap) → a **level** may pick another (Global Scene › Levels › On tap) → a **POI** may pick
  another (Specific Marker › On tap).
- A POI may then **override single fields** (Prefab-style, `_4.1.3` section 2): distance to the marker, tag component,
  action component, focus. Overridden = bold + blue bar + ↺.
- **Buttons per POI:** the list comes from Base elements and the POI's content (no 3D model → no 3D button). The POI may
  hide a button or force one; an "auto" button without content shows greyed with "no content".

### 3.4 Placement (the resolver)

The tap UI is laid out in screen space from the POI's projected position on every frame:
1. The radial ring opens towards the centre of the screen; the tag goes on the far side.
2. Floating panels flip up near the bottom; with no room beside the leader line, the picture goes on top of the text.
3. **Keep inside the screen** (setting): every part moves inside the safe area (below the status bar, above the home bar
   or the screen card); the ring moves as one group.
4. The tag moves off the buttons (below, above, left or right of them) and never covers the POI (right, left, above,
   below the POI, the first that fits).
5. **The screen card makes way for the POI** (setting): when the POI ends up under the card, the card tucks down to its
   title. In landscape the screen card docks on the right, like a maps app.

Tested in the Lab over 3,080 camera poses: phone and tablet, portrait and landscape, near and far, tilted, from the side,
and with a big marker on every style.

## 4. The App screens (App tab)

| Section | Settings |
|---|---|
| 1 Start | splash, friendly loading lines |
| 2 Onboarding | ask the look (Explorer / Family), the language, interests (topic lenses); skippable |
| 3 Main AR screen | search bar top / bottom / off (voice, filter); Ask Azu, Explore and Profile buttons in a corner |
| 4 **Pointing** · off-screen POIs | below |
| 5 On tap | section 3 |
| 6 Explore menu | tours, games, moments on the wall, wall layers |
| 7 Look | Explorer (premium) / Family (cartoon): design tokens per audience (`_3.5`) |

**Pointing.** *The selected POI*, after a tap, when the phone turns away: a pointer at the screen edge (name + arrow, or
an arrow only; tap it to turn back) / close the tap UI / keep the tap UI clamped inside; keep other pointers while a POI is
open. *Other POIs* on the main screen: none / the best few (1-8) / all; pick by level first then the nearest, the nearest
(least turning), or level only; only from level X up; look: name + arrow / arrow / coloured dot; show how far: no /
degrees to turn the phone / metres on the wall (from the point at the centre of the screen). Later: the next stop of a
tour, unseen POIs first.

## 5. Wall-wide experiences (Experiences tab)

| Experience | What | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| **Tours & routes** | named, themed sequences of POIs; each step = a POI + narration; seen / not seen; "next" adapts | Guide narrates; Navigate here; topic lenses |
| **Games** | Find & place; Quiz rally; Treasure hunt (a Guide invitation) | quiz questions per POI; scoring |
| **Moments on the wall** | the 1755 earthquake over the whole wall; day to night | started from the Explore menu |
| **Wall layers** | today's map on the tiles; then and now; time of day / season; under the river; today's landmarks beyond the panel; 360 of the surroundings | the wall geo-reference |
| **Capture & share** | photo / recording, stickers, drawing, share (may become its own domain) | -- |

**The wall's geo-reference** (Global Scene › Wall & content, planned): control points (a wall point ↔ a lat / lon), at
least 4, giving a wall-to-map transform with a fit-quality readout; every POI gets a derived lat / lon. Needed by Today on
the map, the map overlay, today's landmarks and GPS.

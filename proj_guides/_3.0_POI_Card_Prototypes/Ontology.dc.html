Ontology · how blocks are organised

Every block in two taps

Blocks are grouped by the visitor's intent (the question they are asking), not by file type. Format, time and requirements are facets on top, so any nav element can also filter or pivot by them.

L1 · Family · tap 1Media8 fixed intents. Always what the primary nav shows (tabs, dock, grid, FAB).
L2 · Kind · tap 2360° panoramaExtensible list per family. Shown as chips, a sheet, or a sub-grid.
L3 · Block instance“Inside the gatehouse”The actual block. Nav scrolls to it or opens it. Several instances can share a kind.
Collapse rules Empty family → hidden.
Family with one block → L2 skipped, 1 tap.
Kind with one instance → L3 skipped.
More than 5 families in a bar → last slot becomes “More”.

05Rich Text · drop cap · collapse 5 lines

Painted across the last standing gatehouse of the old wall, the Watcher keeps one open eye on the river road. The artist layered stencilled stonework over hand-painted foliage, so the mural seems to grow out of the masonry itself. Up close, the iris dissolves into hundreds of small tiles of colour that only resolve when you step back.

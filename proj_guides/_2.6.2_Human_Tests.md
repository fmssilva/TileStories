# Human Tests -- Select, Filter & Search (Tier 2, device)

Only what a real phone can reveal (`40-testing.md` 4.6). Everything else is covered by `SearchSceneTests` /
`SearchDemoTests` in the Editor. The same steps are in the POI Editor: Global Scene > Select, Filter & Search >
Test > How to Device Test.

## Setup

1. Untick "Add search demo" (a development build would otherwise show the demo).
2. Save All to JSON, Copy to StreamingAssets, Build And Run (USB, or Wi-Fi: `adb pair <ip>:<port>` + code, then
   `adb connect <ip>:<port>`).
3. Logs: `adb logcat -c`, then `adb logcat -s Unity > __logcat.txt`; read the file.

## Checklist

- [ ] **Taps:** a finger tap on a marker opens its card and dims the others; a second tap clears. A small or
      distant marker is still easy to hit; a tap never selects a marker that is fading away.
- [ ] **Zoom on select:** a tap inside a crowd zooms the camera in; a lone marker does not.
- [ ] **Typing:** the phone keyboard types into the search field; results follow without lag; clearing the text
      brings every marker back.
- [ ] **Filters:** every chip is comfortable to tap one-handed; the tray scrolls on a small screen. With the tray
      open and no results, is it obvious that the "Remove filter" button waits under the tray? (it does not show
      until the tray closes -- decide whether the tray should repeat it)
- [ ] **Card over the zoom:** after a zoom-on-select, is the tapped crowd still usable above the detail card?
- [ ] **Views and map:** List / Map / Highlight switch instantly; a map dot tap selects its POI.
- [ ] **Outdoor legibility:** panel text readable in direct sun.
- [ ] **Safe area:** nothing hides under the notch or the home indicator.
- [ ] **Voice:** with Enable Voice Search on and NO speech backend in the build, the mic must NOT appear. (With the
      planned backend added: the permission prompt appears once and a spoken POI name finds it.)
